## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to DKYJ Director (大开眼界导演台)! This application helps you plan and visualize your camera shots before you ever step onto a real set. Think of it as a digital rehearsal space where you can block out scenes, position virtual cameras, and preview your shots using nothing more than your iPhone as a viewfinder.

Whether you're a filmmaker, content creator, storyboard artist, or just someone curious about how movies are planned, this tool makes professional camera previsualization accessible to everyone.

### What Does DKYJ Director Do?

Imagine you're planning a scene. You need to know where the camera should sit, how it should move, and what the audience will see. DKYJ Director lets you:

- Set up virtual scenes with editable blocking (character and object positions)
- Move a virtual camera around like you would on a real set
- Use your iPhone Safari browser as a live preview window
- Save and reuse your favorite scenes
- Connect optional AI assistant tools to help speed up your workflow

The best part? Everything runs locally on your computer. No cloud accounts, no subscriptions, no waiting for uploads.

## 🎯 Your First Project

Once you have DKYJ Director running, here's a quick tour to get you started:

### The Main Interface

When you first open the app, you'll see a 3D viewport in the center of the screen. This is your virtual set. Around it, you'll find panels for controls, scene elements, and settings.

### Setting Up Your Scene

1. **Create a new scene** by clicking "File" or the "+" button (depending on your platform).
2. **Add objects** using the toolbar on the left side. These represent actors, props, or set pieces.
3. **Move objects around** by selecting them and using the movement arrows that appear.
4. **Rotate and scale** objects using the same selection tools.

### Working with the Camera

This is the heart of DKYJ Director. The virtual camera behaves like a real cinema camera:

1. **Select the camera** from the scene elements panel.
2. **Move the camera** just like you move other objects.
3. **Adjust camera settings** (focal length, aperture, etc.) in the properties panel.
4. **Preview your shot** by looking at the camera view window.

### Using Your iPhone as a Viewfinder

This feature sets DKYJ Director apart:

1. **Make sure your iPhone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network.**
2. **Open Safari on your iPhone.**
3. **Type in the address shown in the app's "Connect Device" section.** The app displays a special local address (something like `192.168.1.10:3000`).
4. **Press "Connect".** Now your iPhone screen shows exactly what your virtual camera sees.
5. **Walk around your space** holding the iPhone. Move the phone and watch the camera angle change on your computer in real-time. It's like holding an actual film camera!

### Saving and Reusing Scenes

1. **Save your scene** by going to File > Save or pressing Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac).
2. **Name your scene** and choose a location to store it.
3. **Reopen later** by going to File > Open and selecting your saved scene.

Scene files are small (just a few kilobytes), so you can store hundreds without taking up noticeable space.

## 📝 Troubleshooting Tips

### The app won't start

- Make sure you have the latest graphics drivers installed.
- Try running the app as administrator (Windows) or from Applications folder (Mac).
- Check that your system meets the minimum requirements.

### iPhone won't connect

- Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Check your computer's firewall settings—you may need to allow DKYJ Director to accept connections.
- Try a different browser on your iPhone (though Safari works best).

### The 3D view is laggy

- Lower the graphics quality in the settings (look for "Render Quality").
- Close other programs that use a lot of graphics memory.
- Reduce the number of objects in your scene.
